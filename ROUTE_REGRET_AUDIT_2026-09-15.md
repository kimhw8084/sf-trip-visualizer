The four routes now share a strong 34-place first-trip core, but they are not equally regret-protected and they are not promises to execute every visible marker. A1 remains the default no-regret recommendation. If Yosemite must be two nights, B2 is the safer default. A2 is an intentional higher-effort scenery maximizer. ## Day-by-day audit

### Oct 3 — date-scarce San Francisco opening

Ferry Plaza market/Latine Makers first, then Exploratorium, is still the correct low-friction opening after the prior-night arrival. Bay Lights remains a bonus, not a debt. No regret-critical stop should displace the dated market or the recovery block.

### Oct 4 — Golden Gate / Muir Woods / Presidio

The early Golden Gate Bridge and first Muir Woods reservation window remain the right crowd and parking trade. Muir Woods requires a parking or shuttle reservation and uses a 30-minute arrival window; weekend slots can sell out. The Golden Gate Bridge's southeast and northeast visitor parking lots are closed 11:00–17:00 on weekends/holidays through Oct 12, 2026, so the early bridge slot is materially better. Battery Spencer stays conditional: take it only if parking is immediately visible and conditions are calm, otherwise continue. Tunnel Tops is a worthwhile family recovery stop, not a reason to extend the morning.

### Oct 5 — Alcatraz / compact north-SF icons

Alcatraz remains first-visit essential and should retain the earliest practical departure. The afternoon North Beach/Coit/Lombard/cable-car/Wharf pieces are intentionally conditional; line, hills, naps, and parking decide which survive. PIER 39 sea lions are now explicitly covered. Sausalito is not a missing must: it is acceptable only as an opportunistic short lunch/waterfront stop after Muir Woods with immediate parking, never at the cost of the rest block.

### Oct 6 — Monterey coast

Point Lobos at opening and Aquarium at the closing edge remain strong. The previous 70-minute 17-Mile label was too easy to misread; it now says **three signature stops maximum** (Spanish Bay, Bird Rock, Lone Cypress), not a complete 17-stop tour. Bixby remains a choose-one replacement for both Carmel and 17-Mile Drive, never an addition. Caltrans currently reports one-way control near Rocky Creek through Nov 30, 2026 and a closure much farther south; Bixby is therefore reachable from Monterey but delay-sensitive and must be rechecked.

### Oct 7 — Yosemite arrival

All routes now show the same safe arrival logic: Cook's Meadow/Valley orientation, optional El Capitan Meadow if the babies are awake, recovery, then **Tunnel View or Valley View—one only**. Use Tunnel View for Yosemite West/Wawona lodging and Valley View for Foresta/El Portal lodging. This removes backtracking and repairs the previously disconnected B route geometry.

### Oct 8–9 — Yosemite decision core

Glacier Point is the most irreplaceable high-country target and outranks Mariposa when weather forces a choice. NPS currently lists Glacier Point Road open, while emphasizing variable fall conditions and near-freezing higher-elevation nights. Yosemite Falls is deliberately not promoted as a dedicated stop: NPS reports it and other falls low, and Yosemite Falls is often a trickle or dry by late summer. Bridalveil is visible from Tunnel View; add its easy 20-minute walk only when it is visibly flowing and parking is immediate. A1's flex day is the strongest protection. A2 must convert Mariposa to a Glacier retry after a failed Oct 8. B1/B2 have no comparable weather cushion.

### Oct 9–10 — Fleet Week trade

This is the largest deliberate omission, not an oversight. Fleet Week's official 2026 schedule places the Parade of Ships on Friday Oct 9 and the Air Show Oct 9–11. The workbook scored these events highly for regret but excluded them because the waterfront crowd/noise/parking burden conflicts with two infants and the Yosemite core. If the adults value the Air Show more than the family recovery block, only B1/B2 offer a sensible optional split on Saturday: two adults use transit/walk to a non-ticketed viewpoint while two adults remain with the babies. Do not drive to waterfront parking and do not convert this into a six-person default.

### Oct 11 — Golden Gate Park / final icons

Botanical Garden early and Academy after parking opens remain the strongest family structure. Japanese Tea Garden is a source-universe blind spot, but not regret-critical here; treat it only as a same-area swap for the Botanical Garden if Japanese landscape design matters more to the family. Coit/Lombard/cable car/PIER 39 must remain a pick-the-survivors cluster, not a checklist.

## High-scoring exclusions rechecked

- Fleet Week Parade/Air Show: real regret value, but intentionally excluded from the six-person default because of dated crowd/noise/parking conflict; adult split is the only recommended recovery.
- Point Reyes/Tomales Bay, Filoli, Bay Area Discovery Museum, Henry Cowell/Armstrong/Calaveras redwoods, Big Basin, and Tenaya Lake: all add drive burden or duplicate a stronger included experience. None should displace Muir Woods, Point Lobos, or the Yosemite weather buffer.
- Mission Dolores/Mission District: the most meaningful cultural omission. Add only if California mission history or contemporary neighborhood culture is a declared family priority; it otherwise displaces higher-value first-trip family anchors.
- Sausalito: contained in the workbook's Marin bundle rather than as its own selected stop. It remains a convenience bonus, not a guaranteed destination.

## Operational gates before departure

1. Lock the exact Yosemite lodging area; it determines Tunnel View versus Valley View.
2. Reserve Muir Woods parking/shuttle and download the confirmation before arrival because cellular service is unreliable.
3. Confirm Alcatraz departure, Academy ticket/hours, hotel early luggage drop if considering B1, and rental-car toll handling.
4. Check NPS Yosemite roads/weather/webcams at 72 hours and again the prior evening; carry chains if conditions require them.
5. Check Caltrans SR 1 before choosing Bixby.
6. Keep the deletion rule: if a nap slips 45+ minutes or family energy drops below 6/10, remove the next optional/strong item rather than compressing the day.

## Remaining limitations

- Weather, wildfire, smoke, temporary road closures, special-event operations, and infant condition cannot be guaranteed weeks in advance.
- The map represents planned route logic and locally cached reference geometry, not live traffic navigation.
- “No regret” means the best protected decision structure for this family, not visiting every candidate. Trying to execute every marker would create the largest avoidable regret: rushed days and lost recovery.
